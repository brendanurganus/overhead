        Where this comes from

        Brightness values are estimates from each satellite's typical reflectivity, its distance from you and the angle of sunlight. Real satellites can flare brighter or look fainter than predicted. China's Tiangong station has no catalogued brightness, so it uses an estimate based on observed passes (about magnitude −2 at its best).
